# Smokeshed

> **A burning calendar for the people who breathe the smoke.** NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026.

Instead of drawing a box around fires, the user drops a pin where they live. Smokeshed works out which burning areas actually affect that location's air, its *smokeshed*, and builds a harmonized MODIS + VIIRS burning calendar for that area. A *watershed* is all the land whose rain ends up in your river; a *smokeshed* is all the land whose burning ends up in your lungs.
